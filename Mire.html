Mire plant

Botanical Name: Piper nigrum (L)

Common Name: Black Pepper / Pepper

Family: Piperaceae

Description: A perennial climbing plant with green leaves and small fruits in spikes.

Medicinal Uses

  1. Traditionally used to improve digestion and relieve cough and cold.
  2. Helps in digestion and may reduce indigestion.
  3. May have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.
